## What the numbers say

1. About 58 % of the hybrid's MSE at unseen stations is a constant per-station offset (bias). It is not day-to-day error.
2. A second-stage station-mean model built from the existing v2 satellite/static features does not reduce that offset. Its station-mean errors are 0.94 correlated with the hybrid's.
3. Adding POLARIS 30 m SSURGO-based soil properties (static map product, no in-situ input) reduces it. It is the only change that clears the bootstrap noise floor (about ±0.01 R²) at unseen stations and unseen regions.
4. Median per-station r is essentially unchanged by every v3 change. The gains are in bias and level, not in temporal dynamics.
5. v4: re-extracting POLARIS at the exact NRCS coordinates and fitting the trees on relative wetness (Se) are both null (|ΔR²| <= 0.001 vs the v4 reference). The models use the ~1 km window means, which barely change with the coordinates. The trees already receive porosity as an input.
6. stations.csv coordinates are off by > 1 km at 21 of the 184 evaluated stations. Two are badly off: Starkville 17.7 km, Lovelock 27.5 km. All satellite features there were sampled at the wrong place; re-extracting them needs Earth Engine.
7. v4-3: TempoSAR with POLARIS static inputs beats its own re-run control as a single model (see the TempoSAR rows above). Inside the hybrid it adds only a few thousandths of R² over the v4 reference, and the CI includes 0. The declared v4 candidate stays well above the v2 headline at unseen stations and regions.
8. Daily: the v4 final hybrid, linearly interpolated between Sentinel-1 dates, keeps most of its gain on non-Sentinel-1 days (see the v4 daily tables). Averaging it with the v2 LGBM-daily model is better still, but that row is exploratory.
9. Not done: spatially blocked folds, rolling-origin split, nested stacking, satellite re-extraction at the exact coordinates.
